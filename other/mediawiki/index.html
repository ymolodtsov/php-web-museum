MediaWiki

2007

Released2002
Version shown1.10.2, MonoBook skin
Peak era2005–2010
Written inPHP
DatabaseMySQL
StatusActive

MediaWiki was written to run Wikipedia, and its success there made it the default choice for anyone who wanted a collaborative encyclopedia of their own. The MonoBook skin — a grey sidebar with navigation and search, tabs for Article, Discussion, Edit and History along the top, and a left-aligned Contents box floated inside long articles — is still instantly recognizable as “what a wiki looks like” to anyone who used the web in this period, even on sites with no connection to Wikipedia itself.
