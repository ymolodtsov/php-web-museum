 Linux 2.6.3 Released

Linus has released Linux 2.6.3. This one is mostly fixes: networking and filesystem cleanups, updated drivers for a good number of network cards and SCSI controllers, and the usual round of architecture updates. Marcelo also put out 2.4.25 the same day for those staying on the stable series.
