Picked this one up at lunch today and already spun it twice. "Low Tide" is a lot moodier than their last EP -- way more reverb-heavy, feels like it was recorded in someone's basement at 3am. Track 4 ("Harbor Lights") is probably my favourite so far, the bridge gives me chills.

Anyone else grabbed a copy yet? Curious what the rest of you think about the production on this one.
