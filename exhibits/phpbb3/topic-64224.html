Welcome to phpBB3 -- what's new on the board

by Zane on Sun Aug 03, 2008 6:05 pm

We're back! Thanks for your patience over the weekend while the board was down. Pixel Arena is now running phpBB 3.0.2. All your posts, private messages and settings came across from the old board, and your password still works.

The short version of what's new:

  • User Control Panel -- everything about your account is now in one place (click User Control Panel at the top of any page). Profile, signature, avatar, board preferences, subscriptions.
  • Friends and foes -- add people as friends and they show up at the top of the UCP when they're online. Add someone as a foe and their posts are hidden from you (moderators excepted, sorry).
  • Bookmarks -- bookmark a topic from the bottom of the page and find it again in the UCP. Separate from watching a topic, so no more emails you didn't want.
  • Attachments -- you can now attach screenshots and files to posts instead of hotlinking from Photobucket. 256 KB per file for now, we'll see how the disk holds up.
  • Private messages got folders, rules and drafts.
  • Quick-mod tools -- for the mods: lock, move, split and merge without going through three pages each time. Kestrel is very happy about this.

The new look is called prosilver. The old subSilver look is technically still available, but we're only going to maintain one style, and it's this one. Give it a couple of weeks before you decide you hate it

Bugs, missing posts, broken avatars: reply in the phpBB3 feedback thread in General Discussion, not here.
Pixel Arena admin. Problems with the new board? Post in phpBB3 feedback in General.
