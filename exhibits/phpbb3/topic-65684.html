Core i7 / Nehalem -- November launch, X58 and DDR3 prices

by Trip_Wire on Thu Oct 02, 2008 2:05 pm

Intel's launching Core i7 in November. What's leaked:

  • i7 920 (2.66GHz), 940 (2.93GHz), 965 Extreme (3.2GHz)
  • Prices around $284, $562 and $999
  • New socket (LGA1366), new chipset (X58), triple-channel DDR3 only
  • Hyper-Threading is back

The CPU price is fine, the platform isn't. X58 boards supposedly start at $250-300 and 6GB of DDR3 isn't cheap either.
