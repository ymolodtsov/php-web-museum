Forum rules (updated for phpBB3)

by Zane on Sun Aug 03, 2008 5:50 pm

Same rules as before, a little tidier:

  1. Be civil. Argue about games, not about each other.
  2. No warez, cracks, keygens, or "where can I download" requests. Instant ban.
  3. Search before posting a new topic. Release-date questions go in the release dates sticky in Console Gaming.
  4. Signatures: max 4 lines, no images taller than 100 pixels.
  5. Attachments are for screenshots and files that belong to the post. Don't upload your music collection.
  6. Spoilers for anything less than a month old must be marked. Put a warning in the topic title.
  7. Use the report button for problems. Don't start a thread about it.

Moderators: Kestrel (global), LANwolf (PC Gaming), NorthernLight (Console Gaming), Trip_Wire (Hardware), QuakeWidow (Off Topic).
