Meeting schedule
Written by Dave Reyes   
Tuesday, 02 March 2004
The Riverside Linux User Group meets on the third Saturday of every month. Meetings are free and open to anyone interested in Linux or free software. No membership is required and beginners are welcome.
WhenThird Saturday of each month, 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM
WhereRiverside Public Library, Meeting Room B, 2nd Floor
Next meetingSaturday, 16 October 2004
FormatShort presentation, open Q&A, informal install help afterward
ContactDave Reyes, group coordinator, through the Contact Us page

Bring a laptop if you want help installing or configuring anything. We usually have two or three members on hand with spare burned CDs and an Ethernet hub for the afternoon. The library does not provide wireless access in the meeting room, so plan accordingly.

Upcoming topics include a wireless card compatibility session (see the install-fest write-up for background) and a talk on setting up a home file server with Samba.
