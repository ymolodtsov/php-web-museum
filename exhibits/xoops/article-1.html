Industry News : Unreal Tournament 2004 Ships
Posted by admin on 2004/3/16 10:05:00 (812 reads)

Unreal Tournament 2004 is in stores as of this morning. The regular edition comes on six CDs, and there is also a DVD special edition with a Logitech headset in the box if you want to pay a little extra for voice chat.

The big addition over UT2003 is Onslaught, the new vehicle mode where teams link up power nodes across a big outdoor map. Assault is back from the original game, and all the UT2003 maps come along for the ride.

A few of us played the demo all last week and the netcode feels a lot better than 2003 did at launch. We will have a proper review up once we have spent some time with the full game.
