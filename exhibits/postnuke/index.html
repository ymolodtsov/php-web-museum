Welcome to Northern Sky

Northern Sky is an amateur astronomy club for Duluth, Superior and the North Shore. We meet the fourth Tuesday of the month (September to May) at 7:00 PM and observe whenever the lake lets us. Members and the public are welcome at all of our observing nights. New to the hobby? Read the FAQ, then come to a meeting - you do not need a telescope to join.

Sky Events: Saturn at opposition tonight
Posted by: lynn_s on Thursday, January 13, 2005 - 05:00 PM
Planets 
Saturn is at opposition today, rising at sunset and up all night in Gemini. It is about as bright as it gets, magnitude -0.4, and the rings are tilted wide open. Any telescope will show the rings and Titan.

Tomorrow the Huygens probe lands on Titan. It would be nice to look at Titan tonight and know there is a spacecraft on the way down to it.

Observing Reports: Comet Machholz meets the Pleiades
Posted by: katie_p on Sunday, January 09, 2005 - 09:40 PM
Comets 
Katie writes: "Comet Machholz (C/2004 Q2) passed the Pleiades on Friday and Saturday. I got out to the Rice Lake Road field Saturday night with the 300D on Nate's mount. The comet was naked eye at about magnitude 3.5, a green fuzzy spot a few degrees from the cluster. 20 x 30 second exposures stacked show the thin blue ion tail."

The comet is now heading north through Perseus and stays up all night. Charts are in Downloads.
