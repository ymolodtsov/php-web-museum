Loaner telescopes back in circulation

Club News / Equipment
Posted by mbergstrom on Feb 16, 2005 - 08:15 PM
Mark writes: "The club's two loaner telescopes are back from repair and available for members to borrow for a month at a time. The Orion XT8 Dobsonian has a new focuser and the Meade ETX-90 has new batteries and a fixed declination clutch."

"Sign-out is at the meetings. You need to have been a member for 30 days. The loaner agreement is in Downloads."
