Club News: Welcome to the new Northern Sky website
Posted by: dhalvorsen on Saturday, June 14, 2003 - 12:05 PM
Website 
The club finally has a website of its own. The old page on the university server was three years out of date and only Lynn could update it, so we moved to a hosted account and set up PostNuke.

Members can register an account, post comments under news stories and submit their own observing reports. If something is broken, post a comment here or catch me at the next meeting.
