Sky Events: Messier Marathon: March 11 at Rice Lake Road
Posted by: mbergstrom on Friday, February 18, 2005 - 09:30 PM
DeepSky

Sky Events: Saturn at opposition tonight
Posted by: lynn_s on Thursday, January 13, 2005 - 05:00 PM
Planets 
Saturn is at opposition today, rising at sunset and up all night in Gemini. It is about as bright as it gets, magnitude -0.4, and the rings are tilted wide open. Any telescope will show the rings and Titan.

Tomorrow the Huygens probe lands on Titan. It would be nice to look at Titan tonight and know there is a spacecraft on the way down to it.

Sky Events: Total lunar eclipse: clear skies over Leif Erikson Park
Posted by: lynn_s on Thursday, October 28, 2004 - 07:45 AM
Events 
For once the weather cooperated. The clouds broke up around 8 PM and we had a clear sky for the whole total eclipse last night, which ran from 9:23 to 10:45. Seven club telescopes were set up in Leif Erikson Park and we had around 150 visitors, plus a few dozen more who watched from the Lakewalk.

The Moon went a coppery orange with a brighter lower edge. Saturn was rising in the east during totality and Mark swung over to it for the people who asked what that bright star was.

Sky Events: Transit of Venus at sunrise from Brighton Beach
Posted by: lynn_s on Wednesday, June 09, 2004 - 07:10 AM
Events 
Nine of us were at Brighton Beach by 4:45 yesterday morning to catch the end of the transit of Venus. The Sun came up out of the lake at 5:17 with Venus already close to its edge, a sharp black dot that was visible through the eclipse glasses even with the Sun that low.

Third contact came at 6:04 and Venus was off the disk at about 6:23. Rick's Unitron with a Baader film filter gave the best view. Dave got the black drop effect on his webcam, but only just.
