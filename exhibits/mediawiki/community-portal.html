[edit] How to help

  • Write an article about a computer you know well. Have a look at wanted pages for ideas.
  • Expand an existing article. Short articles are listed in Category:Stubs.
  • Check facts. If you have the original manuals or magazines, please add dates and prices from them.

[edit] Style guide

  1. Use the article name that the manufacturer used (Commodore 64, not C64 or Commodore C-64). Create a redirect for common short names.
  2. Put the {{Infobox computer}} at the top of articles about a computer model.
  3. Use British spelling for British computers and American spelling for American ones. Don't change other people's spelling just because it isn't yours. (See Talk:ZX Spectrum.)
  4. Write in your own words. Do not copy text from Wikipedia, magazines or manuals.
  5. Sign your posts on talk pages with four tildes (~~~~).
