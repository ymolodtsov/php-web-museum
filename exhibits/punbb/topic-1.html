Forum rules - read before posting

1. Search before you post. Most questions have been asked already.
2. Use a descriptive subject. "Help!!!" is not a subject.
3. Post the exact error message, your distro and your kernel version. Put long output in [code] tags.
4. No warez, no serial numbers, no "where can I download" threads.
5. Be civil. Editor flame wars are tolerated in General Programming only.
