Rebuilding a few things with GCC 4.0.2 on an Athlon 64. Is there any point in -O3 for general stuff, or is -O2 -march=athlon64 -pipe where everyone ends up? The Gentoo forums are full of people swearing by flags they never measured.

Re: best compiler flags for -O2 vs -O3

-O3 mostly adds more aggressive inlining and -funswitch-loops, so binaries get bigger, and on a lot of code they get slower because of cache pressure. Use -O2 unless you have a benchmark that says otherwise.

Quick test with lame, same WAV, best of five runs:

Code:

-O2 -march=athlon64                 41.8s
-O3 -march=athlon64                 41.2s
-O3 -march=athlon64 -funroll-loops  42.0s

Within noise. -O2 it is.
