Spring Sketch Swap 2007 - sign-ups open until March 31
Posted by: Quillfeather - 03-12-2007 08:15 PM - Replies (13)

It's back! Same as last year:

How it works

  • Reply here to sign up before Saturday March 31.
  • On April 1 I'll PM everyone the name and mailing address of the person they're drawing for.
  • Draw them something on real paper, any size up to 11x17, any medium. Their characters, your characters, a crossover, whatever.
  • Mail it by April 21. Post a scan in this thread when yours arrives.

International is fine, just be prepared to pay postage. Last year we had 14 people and only one envelope went missing (sorry Pagecount).

Forum rules - please read before posting
Posted by: Quillfeather - 08-14-2005 10:40 PM - No Replies

Welcome to Inkwell! This is a forum for people who make webcomics and people who read them. A few ground rules:


  1. Be decent. Critique the work, not the person.
  2. Show & Tell is for your own comics. One thread per comic, and bump it when you update rather than starting a new one.
  3. No hotlinking huge images. If a page is wider than 800 pixels, post a thumbnail or a link.
  4. Link swaps and ads go in Hosting & Promotion, not in every thread you post in.
  5. Nothing you wouldn't want your mom to see in the main forums. If your comic is adult, say so and link to it.
  6. No warez. Don't ask for a "free" copy of Photoshop or Manga Studio here.


That's it. Mods are inkslinger_dave and me. PM either of us if something's wrong.

- Jess
