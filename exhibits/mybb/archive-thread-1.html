Welcome to Inkwell! This is a forum for people who make webcomics and people who read them. A few ground rules:


  1. Be decent. Critique the work, not the person.
  2. Show & Tell is for your own comics. One thread per comic, and bump it when you update rather than starting a new one.
  3. No hotlinking huge images. If a page is wider than 800 pixels, post a thumbnail or a link.
  4. Link swaps and ads go in Hosting & Promotion, not in every thread you post in.
  5. Nothing you wouldn't want your mom to see in the main forums. If your comic is adult, say so and link to it.
  6. No warez. Don't ask for a "free" copy of Photoshop or Manga Studio here.


That's it. Mods are inkslinger_dave and me. PM either of us if something's wrong.

- Jess
