Own domain if you're serious about it long-term, but there are no readers there until you bring them. A lot of people start on Drunk Duck or SJ and move once they have an archive. If you do, put a big link on the old site pointing to the new one.

Whatever you pick, buy the .com for your title now. It's $9 and you'll regret it if someone else grabs it.
