RE: Brush vs. nib - the inking thread

Load the brush, then wipe most of it off on the side of the jar. Rest your hand on a scrap of paper, pull the line towards you, don't push. And practice on cheap paper, a whole page of just lines and circles every day for a week.

Real ink. Real paper. Real smudges.
