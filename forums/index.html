Forums

Six exhibits covering the bulletin board software that hosted the web's communities before social media absorbed them.

phpBB

The free, open-source board nearly everyone ran at some point. Reconstructed as Pixel Arena Forums, a gaming community.

Version 2.x, subSilver theme · peak era 2001–2007

vBulletin

The commercial standard for large, established communities. Reconstructed as Overclock Hardware Forums.

Version 3.x · peak era 2005–2009

Invision Power Board

A polished commercial alternative with its own distinct visual identity. Reconstructed as a music/entertainment community.

Version 2.x · peak era 2005–2008

Simple Machines Forum

A free board known for a clean, compact interface. Reconstructed as a photography community.

Version 1.x · peak era 2006–2010

PunBB

A deliberately minimal forum that rejected the feature-heavy model. Reconstructed as a programming/Linux community.

Peak era 2005–2008

MyBB

The free board that grew up as phpBB 2 aged, popular with small hobby communities. Reconstructed as Inkwell, a webcomic creators' forum.

Version 1.2 · peak era 2006–2010
