Simple Machines Forum

2007

Released2006 (1.0)
Version shown1.1.3
Peak era2006–2010
Written inPHP
DatabaseMySQL
StatusActive

Simple Machines Forum grew out of the same YaBB community that had produced earlier free bulletin boards, and positioned itself as free, fast and clean at a time when vBulletin charged a licence fee and phpBB2 was starting to look dated. Its compact default theme put board statistics and a news strip right on the index page, alongside a tidy Recent Posts list, and it earned a loyal following among communities — photography clubs especially — that wanted something a step up from phpBB without the visual bulk of vBulletin.
