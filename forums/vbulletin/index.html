vBulletin

2007

Released2000
Version shown3.6.4
Peak era2005–2009
Written inPHP
DatabaseMySQL
StatusActive (commercial)

vBulletin was the commercial, paid alternative to phpBB, and large, established communities paid for it precisely because it scaled: forum statistics in the hundreds of thousands of posts, a reputation system, extensive moderation tools, and a “Currently Active Users” block that made a board feel alive even at 3 a.m. Hardware and gaming enthusiast forums in particular gravitated toward it as they grew, since its permission system and template engine could keep pace with tens of thousands of registered members where phpBB started to show its limits.
