Joomla

2008

Released2005
Version shown1.5.3
Peak era2007–2011
Written inPHP
DatabaseMySQL
StatusActive

Joomla emerged in 2005 when Mambo's core developers split from that project, and quickly became its de facto successor. Version 1.5, released in early 2008, was a near-total rewrite and the version most people pictured when they thought of Joomla: breadcrumb navigation, print/PDF/email icons on every article, a tidy login module, and a two-sidebar layout built from modules around a central component. It became a default choice for small business and design-agency websites throughout the late 2000s, competing directly with WordPress as that platform grew more CMS-like.
