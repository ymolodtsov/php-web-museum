PHP Web Museum > CMS

Content Management Systems

Eight exhibits covering the PHP-based CMS packages that shaped portals, company sites and personal publishing between 2002 and 2010.

PHP-Nuke

Three-column news portal engine. Reconstructed as Open Source Planet, a Linux/software news site.

Version 7.x · peak era 2002–2004

Mambo

Modular CMS, forerunner of Joomla. Reconstructed as a local technology/community organization site.

Version 4.5.x · peak era 2004–2005

Joomla

Mambo's successor. Reconstructed as Orbital Design Studio, a small design/technology company.

Version 1.5 · peak era 2007–2011

Drupal

Node-oriented CMS favoured by technical users. Reconstructed as an open-source university research project.

Version 6 · peak era 2008–2010

WordPress

Blogging software turned CMS. Reconstructed as a personal technology blog running the Kubrick theme.

Version 2.x · peak era 2006–2008

XOOPS

Object-oriented portal system. Reconstructed as a gaming/community portal.

Peak era 2003–2006

e107

Lightweight CMS popular with clan and hobbyist sites. Reconstructed as a gaming clan/community website.

Peak era 2004–2007

PostNuke

The community fork of PHP-Nuke, with the same portal layout and a cleaner codebase. Reconstructed as Northern Sky, an amateur astronomy club portal.

Version 0.7.x · peak era 2002–2005
